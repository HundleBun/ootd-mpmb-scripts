# Epic Paths — Feat Reference

All 8 epic paths from Odyssey of the Dragonlords are available as selectable feats via `OotD_EpicPaths.js`. Take one at character creation. Each path grants a dedicated notes page on your sheet with its full background, heroic tasks, and item options.

Divine Blessings are separate feats taken after completing all heroic tasks for your path (requires DM approval).

Source tag: `OotD-EP`

---

## How to Select an Epic Path

1. Go to the **Feats** section of your sheet.
2. Search for "Epic Path" — all 8 paths will appear.
3. Select your path. A notes page is automatically generated on your sheet.
4. When your heroic tasks are complete, take the corresponding Divine Blessing feat.

## Epic Path Feats

### Cursed One
- **Restriction**: None.
- **Background**: You come from a family cursed for centuries by the Titans. Lift the curse to free your lineage.
- **Adventure Hook**: The Oracle knows who can end the curse.
- **Monstrous Races**: Especially compatible — many monstrous races have been subjected to curses.
- **Heroic Tasks**: End your family's curse; find a family artifact (1 lesser + 1 greater from list).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of the Gods

---

### Demi-God
- **Restriction**: Must be a native to Thylea.
- **Background**: You are the mortal child of Pythor, god of battle. Redeem your fallen father.
- **Adventure Hook**: Summoned by the Oracle due to your fame and divine heritage.
- **Monstrous Races**: Champion of the druids of Oldwood or a tribe of the steppes.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Find your mother; defeat Hexia (Pythor's greatest foe); finish Pythor's greatest weapon (Rod of Lordly Might or Luck Blade).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of Health

---

### Doomed One
- **Restriction**: Must be a native to Thylea.
- **Background**: Born under a fell astrological sign portending a doom of epic proportions. Your parents died under mysterious circumstances.
- **Adventure Hook**: The Oracle knows your doom reflects the doom of the world.
- **Monstrous Races**: One of your ancestors is a Titan (Lutheria or Sydon) rather than a Dragonlord.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Defeat the creature fated to kill you; forge one protective artifact (Robe of the Archmagi, Breastplate of Invulnerability, or Cloak of Invisibility).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of Protection

---

### Dragonslayer
- **Restriction**: None.
- **Background**: A dragon destroyed your childhood. Find it and exact vengeance.
- **Adventure Hook**: The Oracle knows how to find the dragon responsible.
- **Monstrous Races**: Especially compatible — many monstrous races have ancient grudges against dragons.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Kill the dragon responsible; find dragon-slaying items (1 lesser + 1 greater from list).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of Dragon Slaying

---

### Gifted One
- **Restriction**: Must be a native to Thylea. Not recommended for Paladins with the Oath of the Dragonlord.
- **Background**: You have shown remarkable promise since childhood. You want to become a Dragonlord.
- **Adventure Hook**: The Oracle promises the prophecy will put you on the path to become a Dragonlord.
- **Monstrous Races**: Champion of a common or barbarian family rather than a noble family.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Found a new order of Dragonlords (find the Instant Fortress); attune with a Crown of the Dragonlords; find a dragon egg, hatch it, and raise it as your companion.
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of the Dragonlords
- **Extra Feature**: Crown of the Dragonlords (Gifted One) — activate when the DM awards the Crown to add Bond of the Dragonlords and Dirge of the Dragonlords to your spell list.

> See [gifted-one.md](gifted-one.md) for the full dragon companion system guide.

---

### Haunted One
- **Restriction**: Must be a native to Thylea.
- **Background**: Your family vanished without a trace. Friends and neighbors have no memory of them, as if they never existed.
- **Adventure Hook**: The Oracle claims saving the world will give you answers about your family's disappearance.
- **Monstrous Races**: You were the leader of a tribe (centaurs, satyrs, minotaurs, or sirens) that vanished.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Be reunited with your family; reclaim your family name and legacy by tracking down family artifacts (1 lesser + 1 greater from list).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of Magic Resistance

---

### Lost One
- **Restriction**: Must be an explorer from distant lands.
- **Background**: You sailed from a distant land in search of the Dragonlords' treasure but were shipwrecked on Thylea.
- **Adventure Hook**: The Oracle believes you were sent by the gods to fulfill the prophecy.
- **Monstrous Races**: A stranger from across the Forgotten Sea, welcomed by Thylean tribes but longing for home.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Find your surviving crew; find a way to get back home; find the fabled treasures of the Dragonlords (gems + gold + 1 magic item from list).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of Luck

---

### Vanished One
- **Restriction**: Must be an explorer from distant lands. Not recommended for Paladins with the Oath of the Dragonlord.
- **Background**: You are one of the original Dragonlords, missing for almost 500 years after being enchanted by a nymph named Versi (the Oracle).
- **Adventure Hook**: You are the Oracle's favorite pet.
- **Monstrous Races**: Not a Dragonlord — a champion from native tribes defeated by the Dragonlords, seeking to rebuild the order for all peoples of Thylea.
- **Heroic Tasks**: Find out if you have descendants; reclaim your title (attune a Crown + raise a dragon); recover your fabled armor (Boots of Speed, Dragon Scale Mail, or Cloak of the Bat).
- **Divine Blessing**: Blessing of the Dragonlords
- **Extra Feature**: Crown of the Dragonlords (Vanished One) — activate when the DM awards the Crown to add Bond of the Dragonlords and Dirge of the Dragonlords to your spell list.

> See [gifted-one.md](gifted-one.md) for the full dragon companion system guide.

## Divine Blessing Feats

Take the corresponding blessing feat after completing all heroic tasks for your epic path (DM approval required). Blessings appear in the **Class Features column** on your sheet so they are visible during play.

| Blessing | Epic Path | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Blessing of the Gods | Cursed One | You and your direct family are immune to curses, diseases, and the poisoned condition. |
| Blessing of Health | Demi-God | Constitution score increases by 2 (max 22). |
| Blessing of Protection | Doomed One | +1 bonus to AC and all saving throws. |
| Blessing of Dragon Slaying | Dragonslayer | Advantage on attacks against dragons; dragons have disadvantage on saving throws against your spells. |
| Blessing of the Dragonlords | Gifted One / Vanished One | While mounted on a dragon, gain its damage resistances, immunities, and senses. Adult/ancient dragons share legendary resistance. |
| Blessing of Magic Resistance | Haunted One | Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. |
| Blessing of Luck | Lost One | Once per long rest, reroll any one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw (must use the second roll). |

## Spell Added by This Script

### Dirge of the Dragonlords
- **Level**: 3rd — Necromancy
- **Casting Time**: 1 hour
- **Range**: Touch
- **Components**: V, M (gems and coins worth 1,000 gp, consumed)
- **Duration**: Instantaneous
- **Effect**: Return a dead bonded dragon to life with 1 HP. Closes mortal wounds, restores missing parts, neutralizes poisons, and cures nonmagical diseases. Does not remove magical diseases, curses, or similar effects.
- **Classes**: Bard, Cleric, Paladin

> Note: Bond of the Dragonlords is assumed to be defined by the base OotD script and is not redefined here. If both this script and another define Dirge of the Dragonlords, the later-loaded definition takes precedence — verify with the JS console if unexpected behaviour occurs.
